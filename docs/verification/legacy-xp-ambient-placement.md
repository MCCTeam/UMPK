A real velocity packet restores velocity knowledge independently; it does not repair unknown position, membership or authority. Native hanging zero applies only when the actor is eligible to tick. Minecart subtype packets retain their data and shared canonical identity without inventing passenger geometry.

The final collision change separates padded block traversal, actual shape intersection, the selected landing cell and contracted final-body callbacks. Scanning irrelevant water lilies, vines or tall grass no longer invalidates geometry. Soul sand and water lilies retain exact partial collision boxes. Relevant web, soul-sand, slime and hay effects remain unknown when their callbacks are unsupported.
